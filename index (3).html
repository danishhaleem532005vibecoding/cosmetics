Home FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most about ordering, shipping, and returns across Pakistan.

Orders & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash on delivery across Pakistan, as well as debit/credit card and bank transfer at checkout. All online payments are processed through a secure, encrypted gateway.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
Yes — message us on WhatsApp within 2 hours of placing your order and our team will amend or cancel it before it's packed for dispatch.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?
Most orders arrive within 2–4 business days across major Pakistani cities, and 4–6 days to more remote areas. You'll get a tracking link by SMS and WhatsApp once your order ships.
Is delivery free?
Delivery is free on all orders above PKR 3,000. Orders below that threshold carry a flat PKR 200 delivery charge, calculated automatically at checkout.
Do you deliver outside major cities?
Yes, we deliver nationwide through our courier partners, including smaller towns. Delivery times may be a little longer outside major metro areas.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?
Unopened, unused products can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. For hygiene reasons, opened makeup and skincare items cannot be returned unless faulty.
My product arrived damaged — what do I do?
Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and packaging, and we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — no questions asked.

Products

Are your products authentic?
Every product is sourced directly from brands or their authorised distributors. We never stock counterfeit or grey-market items.
How do I know which shade or product is right for me?
Message our team on WhatsApp with a photo or a description of your skin tone and concerns, and we'll recommend the right shade or formula for you, free of charge.
